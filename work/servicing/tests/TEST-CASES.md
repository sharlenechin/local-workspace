# Servicing dashboard - test cases

381 checks in 9 suites. Generated by `run.py --doc`; edit the suites, not this file.

Every check compares the dashboard (its query, or a live Sigma tile) with SQL written independently from the warehouse facts. **Tolerance** is relative: `exact` means to the cent or to the unit.

Back-test any month with `--as-of YYYY-MM`: both sides are capped at that month end. The live suites (6 and 9) cannot be rewound and are skipped in a back-test.

## Known weak spots

- The reconciled flag (case feed within 10% of the servicer report) is the query's own calculation; windows.collections_reconciled_* and controls.dq_reconciled_share.* / dq_not_reconciled.* read the query to build their expectation, so they test the windowing, not the flag. controls.dq_feeds_stopped.* reads the query's feed-stopped flag the same way.
- design.* checks prove the mock is in sync with the query; they re-implement the mock's logic, not Sigma's.
- Ratios are checked to 1e-9 against SQL; Sigma's display rounding (and the delta display on % tiles) is not checked.
- Tiles with no live check of their own: Servicer Cost %, Month Still Loading (a text, checked by design.status_matches_query on the query) and most of the band's status tiles. Their columns are checked in the query (suites 2-5).
- Mutants exist only for the query, not for Sigma formulas; suite 9 covers the formulas by checking every control setting.
- Nothing is checked against a servicer statement or the ledger.
- The chart's future forecast months convert at each currency's newest rate (no future rates exist); live.chart_*_ahead checks that rule, not a rate anyone published. Colours and dashes are not read back from the rendered chart (an export has none): the series order that sets them is checked in the spec, and the look in a rendered PNG.

## 4 · Rolling windows - L1M, L3M, L6M, L12M and cumulative.

The window is the N months BEFORE the current one and EXCLUDES it: for a January
row, L3M is October + November + December. That is what RANGE BETWEEN 3
PRECEDING AND 1 PRECEDING means on a month index. The average divides by the
months that exist, never by N: 40.6% of portfolio-months have a short window.

These live in SQL, not Sigma, on purpose: a Sigma aggregate recomputes over the
rows currently visible, so under a date filter an L12M would silently become
"the last 12 visible months".

## 6 · Live dashboard - what the Sigma tiles actually show.

Suites 1-5 test the query. This one tests the workbook on top of it: each check
exports a tile from the built workbook and compares it to independent SQL. It
catches what SQL testing alone misses - a renamed column that renders 'Unknown
column' as text, a control default that filters the page (the 43-row trap), a
Sigma formula dividing the wrong way, a Switch arm that points at the wrong column.

Which workbook: sigma_build/workbook.json (written by build_workbook.py), so the
suite follows the build. Controls are at their defaults: §2 on both pages and Data Quality
'This month'; the overview Comparison Windows L1M on Portfolio and L3M on Cases (the user's
choice). The overview cards show the latest reporting month whatever that window. Benchmark 'Servicer'.
Skipped by --offline, and by --as-of (a live tile cannot be rewound).
